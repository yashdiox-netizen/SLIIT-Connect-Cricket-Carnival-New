Tournament Rules & Regulations

  1. Each team must have 8 players, including at least 1 girl.
  2. Each match consists of 5 overs per side.
  3. Each bowler can bowl a maximum of 1 over.
  4. An innings ends after 5 wickets or 5 overs.
  5. Wide and no-balls award 1 extra run and an additional delivery.
  6. LBW and free-hit rules do not apply.
  1. Standard cricket boundary rules apply (4s and 6s).
  2. Players can represent only one team.
  3. Teams must arrive 10 minutes before their match.
  4. Umpires' decisions are final. Misconduct may lead to disqualification.
  5. Tied knockout matches will be decided by a Super Over.
  6. The organizing committee reserves the right to make necessary decisions regarding tournament matters.
Registration Requirements

What your team
needs to register

Have these ready before you start — it keeps verification quick and your slot confirmed sooner.

Register Now →
01

8 Players Per Team

Every squad must register exactly 8 players, with at least 1 girl in the team. Full player names and Pathway details are required at sign-up.

02

SIF Requirement

Each player must submit SIF before approval.

03

Valid Student ID

A clear copy of each player's current university student ID card is required for identity verification.

04

Captain's Details

Full name, Pathway, and active contact number for the team captain, who acts as the main point of contact.

05

Valid Contact Number

A reachable phone number for the team is required for match-day updates, schedule changes, and confirmations.

06

Additional Documents

A photo of each player may be requested for the official match-day roster.

Registration Pathway

From sign-up
to match day

Five simple steps take your team from registration to the middle of the pitch.

STEP 01

Submit Registration

Fill in your team and player details through the online registration form.

STEP 02

Document Verification

Organizers check SIF/NB forms and student IDs for every registered player.

STEP 03

Team Approval

Verified teams are approved and given a confirmed slot in the draw.

STEP 04

Confirmation

Captains receive the final match schedule and reporting instructions.

STEP 05

Match Day

Report on time, check in at the ground, and take the field.
